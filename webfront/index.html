01 / PRINCIPLES

Designed to make
room for the work.

Meridian began with Northspline's desktop interface. The shared system keeps its underlying decisions consistent while letting each product set its own tone.

01

Islands on a ground

Work lives on distinct surfaces over a tinted canvas. Structure comes from placement and weight.

02

Accent means intent

One accent marks the active choice, the main action, and keyboard focus.

03

Focus has a place

Pointer, keyboard, and touch states are part of each control, with touch targets sized for fingers.

04

Weight before size

Hierarchy comes from weight and colour. Desktop text stays between 11 and 17 pixels, so dense screens remain calm.

05
AaAa

Contrast is checked

The token generator fails when text, an icon, a control edge, or the focus ring drops below its contrast floor in any brand or theme.

06

Motion explains

Durations, curves, and springs are tokens. Every animation has a reduced-motion fallback.

04 / TOKENS

One source.
Every surface.

Colour, type, spacing, shape, and motion live in one token file. A generator writes the Dart constants and the design documentation from it.

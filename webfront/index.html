01 / PRINCIPLES

Designed to make
room for the work.

Meridian began with Northspline's desktop interface. The shared system keeps its underlying decisions consistent while letting each product set its own tone.

01

Islands on a ground

Work lives on distinct surfaces over a tinted canvas. Structure comes from placement and weight.

02

Accent means intent

One accent marks the active choice, the main action, and keyboard focus.

03

Focus has a place

Pointer, keyboard, and touch states are part of each control, with touch targets sized for fingers.

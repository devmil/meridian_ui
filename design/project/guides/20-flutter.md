# Flutter

Meridian ships as the Flutter package `meridian_ui` (repository `meridian_ui`). Apps depend on it by path or git and never copy its values.

## Set up an app

```dart
import 'package:meridian_ui/meridian_ui.dart';

MaterialApp(
  theme: buildMeridianTheme(brand: MeridianBrands.mimameidr, brightness: Brightness.light),
  darkTheme: buildMeridianTheme(brand: MeridianBrands.mimameidr, brightness: Brightness.dark),
  home: const MeridianGround(child: ProcessExplorerScreen()),
);
```

- `buildMeridianTheme` installs `MeridianTheme` (a `ThemeExtension`) and tunes the Material pieces that stay in use (text selection, scrollbars, tooltips). Splash and ripple are off. The Material `ColorScheme` gets the closest subset (`primary` = `accent`, `surface` = `pane`, `error` = `danger`) but is never the token source.
- Read tokens through `context.meridian`: `.colors` (every colour token in lowerCamelCase: `pane-alt` → `paneAlt`, `series-3` → `series[2]`), `.text` (the ten styles for the active density and brand fonts, coloured `ink`), `.radius` (the brand's scale), `.shadows`, `.density`. Spacing and sizes are constants: `MeridianSpace.space4`, `MeridianSize.islandHeader`. Durations: `MeridianDurations.select`, wrapped in `MeridianMotion.of(context, …)` to honour reduce motion.
- Fonts: the brand names the families, the app bundles them in its own `pubspec.yaml`. JetBrains Mono comes with the package at weights 400, 500 and 700. Use `FontWeight.w400`, `w500` or `w700` for an authentic bundled face; other weights may be synthesized by the engine. The package-qualified family is `packages/meridian_ui/JetBrains Mono`.
- Icons: `MeridianIcon(MeridianIcons.circleCheck)`. App icons join through `buildMeridianTheme(icons: MeridianIconSet(extra: {...}))`. The package's own tests use `MeridianIconSet(package: null)`, because there the icons are root assets.
- `MeridianGround` (and every overlay: menus, dialogs, sheets, panels, toasts) wraps its content in `MeridianTextScope`: `body` text in `ink` and a transparent Material ancestor, so text never falls back to Flutter's red error style and a `MeridianTextField` needs no Scaffold. Wrap other roots (an `Overlay` of your own) in it too.
- App-only tokens (GitLama's diff and lane colours, CrossProton's privacy spark, Mimameidr's live dot and row heat tint) stay in the app as its own `ThemeExtension`, passed with `buildMeridianTheme(extensions: [...])`.

## Build with the primitives

Every component on this page is a `Meridian…` widget. Build screens from them, not from Material widgets: no `ElevatedButton`, `TextButton`, `IconButton`, `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `SegmentedButton`, `TabBar`, `PopupMenuButton`, `ExpansionTile`, `AlertDialog`, `SnackBar`, `Card` or `InkWell`. `MeridianInteraction` is the one hover, press and focus layer for anything custom (a graph node, a thumbnail, a table header). Its builder gets a `MeridianInteractionState`: paint `state.fill(rest:, hoverFill:, ink:)` for rest, hover and pressed (hover plus 4% `ink`, also `meridianPressed(base, ink)`); the focus ring is drawn for keyboard focus only, on the control that has focus; at touch density the hit area grows to 48px around the control. Lists of rows go in a `MeridianList` for the `space-3` inset. Never animate a fill, edge or rule to or from `Colors.transparent`: it is transparent black, and the transition flashes grey. Use `meridianClear(target)` (the target colour at zero alpha). Controls inside rows hover relative to the row: rows publish their fill through `MeridianSurfaceScope`, and a custom table wraps its cells in `MeridianSurfaceScope.withFill`.
